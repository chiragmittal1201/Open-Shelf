Author's Note

Acknowledgements

Writing No One Killed Vaidehi has been more than a story—it has been a journey through silence, chaos, and understanding the shadows people live with.

I owe immense gratitude to everyone who, knowingly or unknowingly, became a part of this process. To the people who shared stories, emotions, and fragments of truth that shaped my thoughts—you made this book breathe.

To my friends and readers who believed in the idea even when it was just a scattered outline on a late night—thank you for your patience and encouragement. Your faith reminded me why stories matter.

To the countless cups of coffee, the sleepless nights, and the moments of self-doubt—each one added something irreplaceable to these pages.

And lastly, to every reader holding this book—thank you for stepping into this world. May it remind you that even in darkness, there’s always a reason behind the silence.

— Chirag Agrawal

About the Author

Chirag Agrawal is a writer from India whose fascination with human psychology and realism drives his storytelling. He is drawn to the complexity of human choices, the consequences of silence, and the shadows people hide behind.

Through his work, he explores the grey areas of morality, the tension between truth and perception, and the stories that linger long after the last page is turned. Each narrative is born from curiosity, observation, and thoughts that refuse to fade.

Author’s Note

Stories are mirrors, not instructions. They reflect the choices we make, the shadows we carry, and the truths we often avoid. Read carefully, think deeply, and remember—what lingers in the mind can be more revealing than what’s written on the page.

Within these pages, you may encounter moments of darkness, glimpses of desire, and echoes of justice. Each character, each decision, is a fragment of a larger human experience. Let the story guide you, challenge you, and perhaps leave you questioning more than it answers.
