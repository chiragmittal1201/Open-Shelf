Chapter 2

Between the Lens & Fire

Vivaan adjusted the focus of his camera lens, his sharp hazel eyes scanning the room as if the perfect shot could unlock something more than just a picture. The day had barely started, yet Kapoor Fashion was already buzzing with restless energy—designers discussing sketches, assistants rushing with fabric samples, and models perfecting their poses. Among the chaos, one figure remained immovable.

Rhea Kapoor.

She stood near the grand showcase area, issuing precise instructions to a team of stylists. Her tailored navy-blue blazer hugged her frame with effortless grace, a stark contrast to the frantic pace around her. Unlike the others, she didn’t rush—she controlled the storm around her.

Vivaan smirked, lifting his camera. Let’s see if the ice queen melts on film.

Click.

The shutter sound broke through the chatter. Rhea’s gaze snapped to him, her sharp brown eyes narrowing. “Was that necessary?”

Vivaan lowered the camera slightly, a teasing glint in his eye. “Couldn’t help myself. The CEO in her natural habitat—powerful, commanding, terrifying.”

Rhea exhaled, turning back to the stylists. “Let me know when you’re done joking and ready to work.”

He chuckled, but there was something in her expression—an exhaustion she tried to hide behind authority. For all her perfection, he wondered when she allowed herself to breathe.

As the morning progressed, their interactions remained a game of light banter and subtle tension. Vivaan noticed how Rhea barely ate, skipping lunch in favor of meetings. She barely acknowledged exhaustion, yet her fingers tapped restlessly against the conference table as the marketing team debated the campaign strategy.

At one point, Vivaan leaned over, lowering his voice just enough for her to hear. “Ever tried eating? I heard it helps humans survive.”

Rhea shot him a side glance. “Some of us don’t have the luxury of leisure.”

“Luxury?” Vivaan smirked, spinning a pen between his fingers. “I’m just saying, an empire is hard to run if its queen faints.”

She ignored him, but he caught the way her lips twitched—just a fraction. Almost a smile. Almost.

The workday stretched late into the evening. The lights in Kapoor Fashion’s headquarters dimmed, but Rhea remained, reviewing the final plans. Vivaan found himself still there too, though he wasn’t sure why. Maybe it was the commitment to the campaign. Maybe it was something else.

At some point, as they stood over a desk, reviewing an ad shoot concept, their hands brushed. A fleeting, accidental touch.

Neither of them moved away.

A moment hung between them, charged—like something had shifted, but neither wanted to acknowledge it.

Rhea cleared her throat, straightening. “We should wrap this up.”

Vivaan nodded, but as she turned away, he grinned. Interesting.

The next morning, Rhea stood at the head of the boardroom table, her fingers lightly tapping against the polished surface. Around her, the marketing team sat, waiting.

She cleared her throat. “The numbers from our current market campaign aren’t what we projected. Engagement is stagnant, and brand visibility is declining in certain demographics. We need a new approach.”

A murmur of agreement passed through the team. Vivaan, seated across from her, leaned back in his chair, watching her with curiosity. She was in full CEO mode now—calm, precise, but beneath that composed surface, he could sense a growing frustration.

One of the senior strategists adjusted his glasses. “We could expand influencer collaborations. Social media partnerships are proving effective in other industries.”

Rhea nodded. “That’s an option, but we need something bolder. Something that makes people stop and pay attention.”

Another executive spoke up. “We could integrate interactive content—campaigns where customers engage directly with the brand.”

Rhea crossed her arms, considering. “That’s worth exploring, but I want everyone to come back with a solid proposal. We need a new campaign, and I want a plan for it.”

The team nodded, shuffling their notes as the meeting wrapped up. As the others exited, Vivaan lingered for a moment, debating whether to speak. Just as he opened his mouth, Rhea surprised him by glancing his way, her tone softer than usual. "You wanted to say something?" The unexpected shift caught him off guard, but before he could respond, she straightened. "Never mind. We'll talk later." And with that, she walked away, leaving Vivaan smirking. Interesting. Vivaan remained seated, studying Rhea.

She was demanding, relentless, but also determined. He could see the weight she carried, and despite himself, he wanted to see how far she would push to prove herself.

She wants bold? This might be interesting.
