Writer.

I write fiction that explores power, intimacy, and the quiet tensions that exist beneath everyday interactions. My stories often focus on characters placed in unequal spaces—professional, emotional, or social—where what remains unspoken carries as much weight as what is openly confronted.

Open Shelf exists as a simple place to read. The work here is shared chapter by chapter, without urgency or performance, for readers who prefer to engage with stories at their own pace. There are no promises beyond finished narratives, shared honestly, and allowed to stand on their own.
