About the Author

Chirag Agrawal

Fiction Writer

I write fiction about people, relationships, and the things that happen between what we say and what we mean. I'm drawn to complicated dynamics, unexpected connections, and characters who don't always understand themselves as well as they think they do.

My stories move across different worlds and relationships, but they share an interest in people, how they think, what they hide, the choices they make, and the moments that change how they see one another. I enjoy exploring these dynamics through character-driven storytelling, where the smallest interactions can reveal more than a conversation ever could.

OpenShelf is where I share my work, one story and one chapter at a time. If something here catches your attention, I hope you'll stay awhile.
